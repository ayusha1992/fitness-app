## Core philosophy (program)
- A **whole-food, plant-based diet** is the main tool for improving insulin sensitivity, reducing
  inflammation and restoring metabolic health.
- IR is linked to more than diabetes: PCOD, stubborn weight gain, fatigue, brain fog and hormonal imbalance.
  It builds up gradually and can be reversed, starting with food, movement and support.
- **Inflammation comes first**: low-grade, silent chronic inflammation disrupts gut, metabolism,
  hormones and immunity. It makes weight loss hard, causes irregular periods, poor blood-sugar control
  and low energy. If it isn't addressed, IR can keep getting worse even on the "right" diet.
- Sustainable healing process, not a crash course. Consistency beats perfection.

## Program structure (program)
- Phase 1, the 4-week **Inflammation Reset**: meals, lifestyle and breathwork to calm the internal fire,
  repair the gut lining and rebalance the immune and hormonal systems.
- Phase 2, 12-week **plant-based IR reversal** meal plan with full recipes.
- Daily morning and evening detox rituals; weekly meditations (English/Hindi) to reduce stress and
  regulate hormones; therapeutic yoga for IR and inflammation; weekly movement plans, including chair
  workouts; beginner and advanced weight training; weekly tracking of health, inflammation and IR markers.
- Non-medical lifestyle coaching. It does not replace doctors. Supplements are suggestions, not prescriptions.

## The plate (applied)
- ½ plate **non-starchy vegetables** (leafy greens, lauki, tori, bhindi, beans, cabbage, cauliflower,
  broccoli, capsicum, cucumber, salad).
- ¼ plate **plant protein**: dals, rajma, chole, lobia, moong/chana sprouts, tofu, tempeh, soya chunks,
  besan, edamame.
- ¼ plate **whole-grain or millet carbs**: jowar, bajra, ragi, foxtail/little millet, brown/red rice,
  whole-wheat or multigrain roti (1-2), oats, quinoa.
- Small amounts of **whole-food fats**: nuts, seeds (flax, chia, pumpkin, sunflower), avocado, coconut.
  Keep cooking oil minimal (about 1 tsp per person per meal). Avoid deep frying.
- Eating order for steadier blood sugar: vegetables/salad, then protein, then carbs.

## Prefer (applied)
- Anti-inflammatory foods: turmeric with black pepper, ginger, garlic, cinnamon, methi seeds,
  amla, berries, guava, citrus, leafy greens, cruciferous vegetables, flaxseed, chia, walnuts, green tea.
- Low-GI fruits, whole and not juiced, eaten with a few nuts: guava, apple, pear, berries, orange,
  papaya (moderate).
- Fermented plant foods for the gut: homemade idli/dosa batter (millet-based is better), kanji,
  plant-based curd.
- Water 2.5-3 L/day; herbal teas; jeera/ajwain/methi water as morning ritual.

## Limit or avoid (applied, because the program is plant-based and anti-inflammatory)
- Added sugar, jaggery in large amounts, sweets, mithai, sweetened drinks, packaged juices, honey in excess.
- Refined flour (**maida**): white bread, naan, biscuits, cakes, pav, noodles, pasta, pizza base.
- White rice in large portions; poha/upma made mostly of refined grains without vegetables or protein.
- Fried foods: pakoras, samosa, puri, bhature, chips, namkeen.
- Ultra-processed and packaged foods, instant mixes, processed "health" bars.
- Animal products (meat, eggs, dairy: milk, paneer, cheese, ghee, butter, cream). The program is
  plant-based. Swap in tofu for paneer and plant milks (unsweetened soy/almond/oat) for dairy.
- Excess refined seed oils and vanaspati; alcohol.
- Large late-night dinners.

## Meal timing (applied)
- 3 balanced meals; snack only if truly hungry (fruit + nuts, roasted chana, sprouts chaat, makhana).
- Early, light dinner, ideally 2-3 hours before sleep. Aim for a 12-14 hour overnight gap.
- 10-15 minute walk after meals blunts glucose spikes.

## Healthy Indian swaps (applied)
- White rice → millet / brown rice / cauliflower rice, or half portion + more veg.
- Maida roti/naan → jowar/bajra/ragi bhakri, multigrain roti.
- Paneer → tofu; dahi → soy/peanut curd; milk tea → herbal/green tea or tea with unsweetened plant milk.
- Poha/upma → vegetable-loaded with peanuts/sprouts, or besan/moong chilla.
- Biscuits/namkeen → roasted chana, makhana, nuts, fruit.
- Sweets → dates (1-2) with nuts, fruit with cinnamon, chia pudding with plant milk.
- Fried snacks → air-fried or steamed (dhokla, idli, sprouts chaat).

## Lifestyle levers (program + applied)
- Sleep 7-8 h; stress management (meditation, breathwork) because cortisol worsens IR and causes water retention.
- Movement every day (walking, yoga); 2-3x/week strength training builds muscle, which improves insulin sensitivity.
- Weigh at the same time every morning: after the bathroom, before food and water, same scale, similar clothing.

## Interpreting daily weight changes (applied)
- Daily swings of 0.2-1.0 kg are mostly water and gut content, not fat. 1 kg of fat ≈ 7,700 kcal.
- Common causes of a gain on the scale: salty, packaged or restaurant food the day before; high refined-carb meal
  (glycogen holds water); late or heavy dinner; constipation; low water intake; poor sleep; stress;
  menstrual cycle (luteal phase / before periods); new or intense workout (muscle repair water); alcohol;
  weighing after eating or drinking, or at a different time.
- Look at the 7-day trend, not one day.
